Cabal: Diplomacy with receipts

Play classic Diplomacy in the browser. Seven great powers, simultaneous orders, and a DATC-complete Rust adjudicator that explains every bounce, dislodge, and convoy. Cabal is built for humans and AI agents: the sandbox lets you drive every power yourself, while the open-source engine and protocol support bots that need legal orders and explainable outcomes.

When to use this

  • You want a DATC-faithful Diplomacy adjudicator in WebAssembly.
  • You are building an agent that plays or studies Diplomacy.
  • You want to try classic Diplomacy in a browser without a desktop client.
